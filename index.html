        How the score is worked out

        Three things are counted for each person: nights, weekend days worked, and total shifts.

        Each person's fair share is the team's total split in proportion to the days they could work (days not on leave). With no leave, that is simply the team average.

        For each of the three, add up how far every person is from their fair share. Shifts come in whole numbers, so some of that gap cannot be avoided (85 nights among 12 people means someone does 8); that part is not counted. Divide what is left by the team total. The part score is 100 minus that as a percentage, and never goes below 0.

        The overall score is 40% nights, 40% weekend days and 20% total shifts, rounded to a whole number. 100 means as even as whole shifts allow.

        How it works

        1. Set up. Enter the team, dates, shift times and rules. Each section changes only when you press its Set button.

        2. Generate. fairshift fills each day in turn, giving each place to the eligible person who has done the least of that kind of work. A balancing pass then swaps shifts within a day when that makes the roster fairer without breaking a rule. The same inputs and seed always give the same roster; Regenerate tries a new seed.

        3. Check and share. Click any cell to change it. Broken rules are flagged on the cell and listed under the grid. Export a CSV or print it for the notice board.

        Limits. This is a planning aid. It does not check labour-law compliance; the limits are the ones you enter. The method is a heuristic, so it does not promise the fairest possible roster, and if it cannot find one it says so instead of breaking a rule.
